Completed my Business Sales Data Analysis project as part of my Future Interns internship task.

I analyzed the Sample Superstore dataset to understand sales, profit, product performance, regional performance and trends over time.

Key outcomes:
• Analyzed 9,994 transaction records across 2014–2017.
• Total sales: $2,297,200.86
• Total profit: $286,397.02
• Overall profit margin: 12.47%
• Identified Technology as the leading category by profit and highlighted loss-making sub-categories for further review.
• Built an Excel dashboard and prepared a business analysis report with actionable recommendations.

Tools used: Python, pandas, Excel and data visualization.

This project helped me practice data cleaning, KPI analysis, trend analysis, dashboard development and business storytelling.
